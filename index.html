Parent Teacher Council (PTC) Fund Guidelines

The Parent Teacher Council (PTC) at GHS Kotki Bala Hangu is constituted under the Khyber Pakhtunkhwa Elementary & Secondary Education Department regulations to ensure community participation, transparent fund utilization, and institutional development.

Permissible Heads of PTC Expenditure:
  • Minor Civil & Sanitary Repairs (classrooms, toilets, water supply).
  • Stationery & Teaching Learning Materials (registers, whiteboards, markers, chalk).
  • Student Furniture repair and replacement (desks, benches, chairs).
  • School equipment, emergency electric repairs, and co-curricular materials.
  • Needy and deserving student welfare support.
Auditing & Record Keeping Requirements:
  • Every expenditure must have an approved resolution signed by the Chairman and members.
  • A sequential Voucher/Receipt Number must be allocated to every rupee received or spent.
  • All payments above prescribed thresholds require authentic GST/NTN vendor cash memos.
